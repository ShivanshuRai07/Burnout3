    how it worksOne loop, every month

    1. 01Assess7-question CBI work survey, scored 0–100. 50+ is high.
    2. 02PredictForecast next month’s score from this month’s survey and work data.
    3. 03Simulate500 possible paths for no change, less overtime, or support.
    4. 04InterveneA manager logs the support, who owns it, and when it ends.
    5. 05VerifyThe next survey says if it helped, beyond normal noise.
