Individual vs systemic (three reference points)

Absolute (CBI ≥ 50, hours ≥ 48), division (far above colleagues), personal (rising vs own last 3 months). A division is systemic when significantly more people score high than the reference population, or its median week exceeds the hours limit. Groups under 5 are hidden.
